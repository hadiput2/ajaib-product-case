02 · Diagnosis

A signal, not a verdict.

The cohort data localizes where to look. It does not prove whether the cause is product friction, partner handoff, instrumentation, policy, or customer mix.

What the data says
Approval-to-deposit is the major deterioration.

Its conditional conversion fell 17.6 percentage points, versus 9.0 points at KYC submission and 2.1 points at approval. That is enough to sequence investigation—not enough to name a root cause.

Check first

Is the drop real?

Reconcile partner and Ajaib event counts, timestamps, cohort definitions, late-arriving events, and duplicate/user identity rules.

Then segment

Where is it concentrated?

Partner, integration version, platform, app version, bank rail, acquisition source, risk tier, geography, and time of day.

Decision enabled

Contain, fix, or learn?

Choose between traffic throttling, messaging or retry recovery, a targeted integration fix, or a guarded experiment.

  • Event loss or schema driftTest: raw logs vs. warehouse vs. partner aggregates
  • Timestamp or 14-day window mismatchTest: recompute from immutable event times
  • Identity join failureTest: orphaned partner and Ajaib IDs by day
  • Partner UX or validation regressionTest: step-level exits, errors, replayed sessions
  • Eligibility or policy changeTest: rule/version comparison and reject reasons
  • API latency, timeout, or payload failureTest: p50/p95, codes, payload completeness
  • Approval status not reaching the userTest: notification send/delivery/open and status lag
  • Broken deep link or account handoffTest: link routing, authentication, app-open funnel
  • Deposit rail or instruction frictionTest: bank/rail failures, screen exits, support contacts
  • Different intent or cohort mixTest: normalized conversion by acquisition segment

04 · Two-week response

Stabilize, explain, recover, decide.

Redirect the available squad to the highest-information and highest-reversibility work first. Reallocate other squads only if the evidence shows a broader, material platform failure.

  • Verify funnel definitions
  • Guardrail dashboard
  • Contain severe failure if found
Days 2–3

Days 4–8

  • Cohort readout

Resource allocation: restore, reroute, or repair

Decision logic
KNOWN REGRESSIONRestore the working stateIf an old deep link worked, prefer a tested one-day restoration to an untested five-day redesign; deploy with a guarded ramp.
DEPENDENCY FAILUREAssess compliant alternativesPartner rollback, supported alternative flow, or compatibility fix. Reusable capability matters when effort, risk and recovery are comparable.
POST-APPROVAL STABLEStop ongoing KYC leakageDeploy the squad upstream if the KYC submission defect is confirmed and recoverable.
IN PARALLELRecover stranded usersData + Ops/CRM identify eligible users and prepare state-aware reactivation without automatically taking the squad off the upstream fix.

Business continuity is urgent within non-negotiable compliance, customer-safety and data-integrity constraints. Reallocating other squads requires explicit trade-offs.

07 · Difficult launch decision

Would I launch a fix before the full 14-day outcome matures?

Yes—but only as a guarded, reversible release when the causal evidence and early indicators are strong enough.

Decision rule

Ramp on evidence of mechanism. Scale on evidence of outcome.

I would start a limited ramp when

The defect is reproduced or the chosen hypothesis is strongly supported, the change is reversible, compliance signs off, and leading signals improve without new harm.

Start with the affected segment, not all partner traffic
Monitor technical success, status delivery, resume rate, and complaints
Keep a named rollback owner and explicit stop threshold

I would hold or roll back when

Counts remain unreconciled, the mechanism is unknown and the change is irreversible, severe errors move upstream, or customer/compliance risk rises even if conversion improves.

Do not trade a cleaner metric for hidden customer harm
Do not declare recovery on partial-window data
Review mature 14-day cohorts before broad scale

Restore the baseline. Build resilience. Then improve beyond it.
