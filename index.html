02 · Diagnosis

Conversion tells us how many. Latency tells us how long.

The cohort localizes the symptom. It does not identify the mechanism. Time alignment, processing delay & audience intent can change the interpretation.

Observed signal
Approval-to-deposit deteriorated most.

−17.6 pp vs. −9.0 pp at KYC submission & −2.1 pp at approval. Investigate here first, but do not assume the failure begins here.

Case clock

14 days start at registration.

Later approval leaves fewer observed days to deposit. Conditional conversion can fall even if behavior after approval is stable.

Competing explanation

Did the audience change?

Segment by partner, campaign, acquisition promise & intent. A larger, lower-intent cohort can change the funnel without a technical break.

Decision principle

Shift upstream when the clock explains the loss.

If approval slowed but aligned post-approval conversion is stable, investigate upstream before assigning the squad.
